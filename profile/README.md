ProtoGen offers consulting, project development, and workforce development services in markets across the US. We help clients work through a changing energy landscape with solutions for their business, resilience, and sustainability challenges.

Our work covers the full energy project lifecycle: conceptual development, feasibility studies, design and engineering, the RFP process, project management, and operations and maintenance.

## What we do

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Microgrid Development** | Microgrids keep the power on during grid outages from natural disasters, cyberattacks, and other threats. They can also lower OPEX, generate revenue, support EV infrastructure, and reduce carbon. |
| 🎓 **Education & Workforce Development** | As energy systems change, so do the skills needed to build them. We design curricula, run train-the-trainer programs, supply lab equipment, and build learning management systems for national programs such as **ESAMTAC** and **EVITP**. |
| 📊 **Energy Tool Suite** | Intuitive energy planning software, a one-stop shop to upload and analyze data, compare energy system scenarios, and build an action plan. |
| 🔬 **Cleantech Commercialization** | Helping new energy technologies get from the lab to the field. |

## Our tools

- **[Grid Navigator (GridNav)](https://www.protogen.com/tools)**: portfolio-scale energy analysis and financial modeling, made simple.
- **[Microgrid Navigator (MGNav)](https://www.protogen.com/tools)**: advanced data analysis to scope and scale microgrid projects.
- **[HybridFAST](https://www.protogen.com/tools)**: Hybrid System Financial Advisory Spreadsheet Tool, with simple inputs and complete financial visibility.
- **[RE-Mixer](https://www.protogen.com/tools)**: Renewable Energy Mix Tool for regional energy transition modeling and portfolio optimization.
